## About

The **Pamoja Foundation, Inc.** is an independent **501(c)(3)** nonprofit organization established by alumni of the **Epsilon Psi Chapter of Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity, Inc.** at the **University of Maryland, College Park**.

Named in honor of the **Pamoja Assassins**, the charter line that founded Epsilon Psi in 1972, the Foundation was created to ensure that the chapter's enduring commitment to scholarship, leadership, brotherhood, and service continues to impact future generations.

The Foundation exists to:

- 🎓 **Provide Scholarships** for deserving students pursuing higher education.
- 🤝 **Develop Leaders** through mentorship, leadership programming, and professional development.
- ❤️ **Serve Communities** by supporting meaningful service initiatives and outreach programs.
- 📖 **Preserve Our Legacy** by protecting and celebrating the history of the Epsilon Psi Chapter.

---

## Why the Foundation Was Created

### Celebrating 55 Years

In 2027, the Epsilon Psi Chapter celebrates its **55th Anniversary**. As alumni prepare for this milestone, the Foundation provides the charitable infrastructure needed to support anniversary programming while creating a lasting organization that extends far beyond a single celebration.

### Building for the Future

The Pamoja Foundation is not a temporary initiative. It was established to become the permanent philanthropic partner of the Epsilon Psi alumni community, supporting scholarships, educational initiatives, leadership development, and charitable giving for generations to come.

### Expanding Opportunities

As a recognized **501(c)(3)** organization, the Foundation can pursue charitable contributions from individuals, corporations, and grant-making organizations that may only support qualified nonprofit organizations.

### Sustainable Giving

The Foundation utilizes modern fundraising platforms, including **Zeffy**, allowing supporters to contribute with zero platform processing fees so that more of every donation supports the Foundation's mission.
